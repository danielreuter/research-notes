# RTX 4090 pods (RunPod, veritor-campaign)

## CPU quota: ~10 cores, not the 96 `nproc` shows
Measured on vy-integration (97zxgf1oii4cga, 1x RTX 4090, $0.74/h), 2026-09-24 01:45Z, lane integration:

~~~sh
cat /sys/fs/cgroup/cpu/cpu.cfs_quota_us /sys/fs/cgroup/cpu/cpu.cfs_period_us   # 1020000 100000 -> 10.2 cores
grep throttled /sys/fs/cgroup/cpu/cpu.stat                                     # 75 % of periods throttled under load
~~~

`nproc`, `os.cpu_count()` and torch all see 96 cores, so every Python process sizes its OpenMP / intra-op pools for 96.
Consequences seen (integration report, 20260924T0100Z-report-integration.md):
* Concurrent jobs crawl: with 4 gates + 4 pytest processes + 1 bench, a bf16-ampere gate took 1219 s (about 70 s alone) and a
  v3x4 bench stalled for 17 min. `vmstat` showed ~145 runnable threads with 82 % "idle", which is the quota's signature.
* Gate `gate-vu` checks every honest sub-batch against the CPU Python hint reference, which is single-thread CPU-bound:
  fp8-ada-v3 at 4096 VUs took 2316 s beside other jobs; fp8-ada-v3x4 at `--batch 4096` (49 honest sub-batches) did not finish in 50 min.
* The full `backends/direct/ligero` pytest (449 tests) does not finish in about 1 h beside gates.

What to do: run one heavy job at a time. When you overlap correctness-only jobs, set `OMP_NUM_THREADS=2 MKL_NUM_THREADS=2`.
Take timings only with `nvidia-smi --query-compute-apps=pid --format=csv,noheader` empty (coordinator rule, 2026-09-24 01:27Z).

Since lane ops-tools (`lane/ops-tools`, merged after the device wave; kb/ops-tools.md):
* `pod_bootstrap.sh` puts `OMP_NUM_THREADS = MKL_NUM_THREADS = OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS = VY_CPU_THREADS = floor(quota)` in env.sh.
  It also caps its own instance-build procs at the quota. On chr1s1sfq2yeyf (US-TX-3, quota 13.6 of nproc 128), torch's
  intra-op pool was 13 after `source env.sh`, and GNU `nproc` printed 13 because it honours `OMP_NUM_THREADS`.
  torch's inter-op pool still says 128.
* `research pods health <pod>` (also run at the end of bootstrap) checks the pod before you trust its timings.
  4090 reference: SIMT encode l=4096 x 2048 rows 0.330 ms, bf16 8192^3 matmul 160.4 TFLOP/s. Beside a second GPU process it
  measured 7.6x and 2.2x slower and printed DEGRADED.
* Every `run.py bench` / `bench-vu` result now carries `contention`. The same fp8-ada bench-vu (512 VUs) took 0.0301 s clean,
  0.0619 s beside a GPU hog (contended: other GPU process), and 0.0331 s while the host's load average was 148 on 128 cores
  (contended: other tenants; our container was idle). The host load on that pod went from 23 to 148 within 6 minutes.
A +shared (`included-hash-shared`) prover at 4096 VUs, l=16384, p4 needs about 15 GB of the 24 GB. It OOMs beside two gates.

## Ligerito --zk at 4096 VUs needs expandable segments (11-coin LGSC0004)
Since sumcheck-3's 11-coin default (58e76e5d), `run.py bench --relation fp8-ada --total-vus 4096 --zk` OOMs on a 4090 at the
LGSC0004 opening fold: it asks for `T` (3, D, n0) int32 = 9.00 GiB while 6.78 GiB is allocated and 8.04 GiB sits reserved but
unallocated (fragmented segments left by the z_to_f drop), so the allocator's release-and-retry cannot help.
`export PYTORCH_CUDA_ALLOC_CONF=expandable_segments:True` fixes it (peak 15.69 GiB, 0.595 s; non-ZK timing unchanged, 0.404 vs
0.401 s). relation-3's 12-coin runs fit without it. Source: ligerito-2pass-2 report, art:af97c8ab (`oom-1/` has the OOM logs).
My 4090 (v1z7ar00oxtsw7, EU-RO-1, 2026-09-24): cfs quota 1360000 = 13.6 cores, nproc 32.

## fp8-ada Table 2 numbers and gotchas (wave-4090-2, 2026-09-24 04:30Z, main 24f252b1, vy-wave-4090 EU-RO-1, quota 13.6 cores)
* Bare, 4096 VUs, zk interactive: fp8-ada-v3x4 fused l=4096 **p8** is the fastest candidate (local 0.0907 s, same-DC live
  0.1008 s; p4 0.0953 / 0.1020; v3 fused l=16384 p4 0.113; v1 p4 0.169). Committed `included-hash-shared --tile 64x64` v1 p4:
  0.2424 / 0.2627. Same-DC live tax ~1.1x is the prover's own serialize/send, not network wait. art:fb4934af, art:6e2c0d79.
* A live verifier on an RTX 2000 Ada pod ($0.24/h) in the prover's DC works (no CPU-pod stock in EU-RO-1); in-session RTT 1-4 ms.
* fp8-ada-v3x4 `gate-vu --vus 1024 --batch 4096` alone takes 189 s (4 honest / 99 negatives / 0 F; art:42a626da).
* The 1-4 % phase-sum overshoot bench.summary flags on fused v3/v3x4 rows came from the runners, not the fused path: they
  reported the per-key median of each bucket over the reps, which mixes reps when one or two timed passes are slow. From
  lane/fused-phases 9989797f, t.total and every t.* bucket come from ONE rep, the one with the median prover time
  (`phases.median_rep`). Rows produced before that may still overshoot. Source: fused-phases report.
* Same five cells re-run at 9989797f on another 4090 (vy-fused-phases 3tvd7rspgbn8e1, quota 10.2 cores, nproc 64, 06:00Z,
  not contended): v3x4 p8 0.0963 / p4 0.1050, v3 p8 0.1398 / **p4 0.1291**, v1 p4 0.1799 (art:06be3b23 art:91c62994
  art:7f294d16 art:d0789c44 art:2d685314). 4090 hosts differ by ~5-10 % on the same code (v3x4 p8 0.0907 on EU-RO-1), and
  v3's best depth flipped: compare cells only within one pod. A v3 / v3x4 bench-vu takes ~180 s wall: its untimed warm-up
  sub-batch spends ~156 s in `hints_host` (v1's: ~42 s in `witness`); timed rep 1 is still ~4x slower than reps 2-5.
