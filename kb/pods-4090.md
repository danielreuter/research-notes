# RTX 4090 pods (RunPod, veritor-campaign)

## CPU quota: ~10 cores, not the 96 `nproc` shows
Measured on vy-integration (97zxgf1oii4cga, 1x RTX 4090, $0.74/h), 2026-09-24 01:45Z, lane integration:

~~~sh
cat /sys/fs/cgroup/cpu/cpu.cfs_quota_us /sys/fs/cgroup/cpu/cpu.cfs_period_us   # 1020000 100000 -> 10.2 cores
grep throttled /sys/fs/cgroup/cpu/cpu.stat                                     # 75 % of periods throttled under load
~~~

`nproc`, `os.cpu_count()` and torch all see 96 cores, so every Python process sizes its OpenMP / intra-op pools for 96.
Consequences seen (integration report, 20260924T0100Z-report-integration.md):
* Concurrent jobs crawl: with 4 gates + 4 pytest processes + 1 bench, a bf16-ampere gate took 1219 s (about 70 s alone) and a
  v3x4 bench stalled for 17 min. `vmstat` showed ~145 runnable threads with 82 % "idle", which is the quota's signature.
* Gate `gate-vu` checks every honest sub-batch against the CPU Python hint reference, which is single-thread CPU-bound:
  fp8-ada-v3 at 4096 VUs took 2316 s beside other jobs; fp8-ada-v3x4 at `--batch 4096` (49 honest sub-batches) did not finish in 50 min.
* The full `backends/direct/ligero` pytest (449 tests) does not finish in about 1 h beside gates.

What to do: run one heavy job at a time. When you overlap correctness-only jobs, set `OMP_NUM_THREADS=2 MKL_NUM_THREADS=2`.
Take timings only with `nvidia-smi --query-compute-apps=pid --format=csv,noheader` empty (coordinator rule, 2026-09-24 01:27Z).
A +shared (`included-hash-shared`) prover at 4096 VUs, l=16384, p4 needs about 15 GB of the 24 GB. It OOMs beside two gates.

## Ligerito --zk at 4096 VUs needs expandable segments (11-coin LGSC0004)
Since sumcheck-3's 11-coin default (58e76e5d), `run.py bench --relation fp8-ada --total-vus 4096 --zk` OOMs on a 4090 at the
LGSC0004 opening fold: it asks for `T` (3, D, n0) int32 = 9.00 GiB while 6.78 GiB is allocated and 8.04 GiB sits reserved but
unallocated (fragmented segments left by the z_to_f drop), so the allocator's release-and-retry cannot help.
`export PYTORCH_CUDA_ALLOC_CONF=expandable_segments:True` fixes it (peak 15.69 GiB, 0.595 s; non-ZK timing unchanged, 0.404 vs
0.401 s). relation-3's 12-coin runs fit without it. Source: ligerito-2pass-2 report, art:af97c8ab (`oom-1/` has the OOM logs).
My 4090 (v1z7ar00oxtsw7, EU-RO-1, 2026-09-24): cfs quota 1360000 = 13.6 cores, nproc 32.
