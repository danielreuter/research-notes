## Build and the SASS gate

## The card

- There is no packed FADD: `add.rn.f32x2` lowers to two FADDs (`fp8-mainloop.md` 09:20Z; `assessor.md` 08:30Z). (`r20260930-080124-a89c`, `r20260930-081301-747b`)
- An empty-barrier release needs `fence.proxy.async.shared::cta` before its arrive, or the next TMA refill lands under an `ldmatrix` still reading it (unroll 2: 25 of 40 runs wrong). Unroll 1 looked clean only because ptxas waited on every scoreboard before the release (`fp8-mainloop.md` 08:50Z). (`r20260930-084600-778b`, `7b417ee7`, `r20260930-085511-2370`)
- After a warp-role split only the live warps may sync: use a named barrier (`bar.sync 1, 256`), not `__syncthreads` (`fp8-mainloop.md`, Interface). (`356313e9`)
- `setmaxnreg` budgets: a producer at 24 registers leaves the consumers 240, at 40 leaves them 232; hash warps need 56, where v1 spills and v2 doesn't (`1-pearl-c-sm120.md` 15:45Z; `fp8-mainloop.md` 08:07Z). (`cb272bf1`, `282fd3d0`)
- Under nvcc 13.0 the v1 mainloop needs `tile<1>`: `tile<2>` spills (`1-pearl-c-sm120.md`, Answers 4).
- A 160-byte forming row stride has no bank conflicts; 144 bytes is 2-way (`1-pearl-c-sm120.md` 12:56Z). (`r20260930-101643-6b20`, `r20260930-120753-303f`, `r20260930-124211-a304`, `84b953ad`)
- 128-byte-row (WIDE) epilogue stores cut STG L1 tag requests from 16 to 4 (`nvfp4-mainloop.md` 13:08Z). (`10c2b0c4`, `549313d4`)
- Folding the BF16 epilogue into the next tile cost 2.2%: the stage release's MEMBAR waits on the step's global stores (`nvfp4-mainloop.md` 12:40Z). (`24c9aee5`, `3f7b3edf`, `3700375c`)
- BK = 64, seven shallow stages, was 23% slower than 128-deep stages (`nvfp4-mainloop.md` 11:35Z). (`r20260930-111405-3bf7`, `3c598d73`)
- NVFP4 forming is DRAM-bound (about 1.2–1.4 TB/s), so instruction-level changes to it don't show (`5-fp4-design.md` 12:15Z). (`art:b4528fb3c05a3846f318f27a76ca9d9a356610f51ade6189816b0f955fc6bf1e`)
- `rcp.approx` is exact on all 126 valid UE4M3 scales (`5-fp4-design.md` 12:15Z). (`art:c0743bbfb6eb2d66be800f9d9c72297aa99add0a80b3afa4885a54f625a4c469`, `f66c3920`, `r20260930-112248-d1ec`)
