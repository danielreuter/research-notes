[kernel tooling report](kernel-tooling-report.md) proposed (section D). **326 techniques**: 170 kept, 81 dropped, 44 open, 31 superseded. Kernel tooling (bc-1a23b70c) compiled it; no GPU was used.

**How it was built.** Read-only extraction passes over the lane records, one row per technique, merged here: the FP8 kernel
lanes (GPU 0, GPU 1, the FP8 mainloop worker), hashing (GPU 2, bc-b139c29c), the harness and the MVP (bc-0de2d624, bc-dd22acf8,
ops), the attacker and assessor probes (GPUs 3 and 7, bc-d7d4b0d1), the FP4 lanes (GPUs 4 and 5, the NVFP4 mainloop worker), and the panel with `server.md`. Where the panel
and a lane describe one technique they are one row: the lane's kernel-level numbers first, then the panel's whole-call ones.
A later source's status wins. Sources: `internal/pouw/panel/attempts.jsonl`, `internal/pouw/rtx-pro/server.md`, the workers' status
files under `internal/pouw/rtx-pro/workers/`, the lane notes they cite, and the kernel-ledger lineage below.

**Reading a row.**
- **Status:** **kept** (in the kernel or harness now), **dropped** (tried, not kept), **open** (undecided, waiting or running),
  **superseded** (replaced by a named later technique).
- **Effect:** the numbers exactly as the source gives them, with its label where it has one (Measured needs a run; Estimated
  and Derived don't). "On the panel:" starts the panel's whole-call figures.
- **Not isolated:** a technique timed only inside a larger change says so. Two to keep in mind: the LATE release shares its only
  run (`r20260930-082731-bebf`) with the dropped skewed schedule, and `form_rows` was timed only with the `p,one` and `p,fold` forms.
- **Runs** are `research` run ids and `art:` ids in the evidence store; "no run" means priced or proposed, not measured.

**What to do with it.** Before starting a technique, search this page for it (and its mechanism tag). If it's here, start from its
row's evidence and reason. Later versions are generated from the `kernel-attempt/v1` records' mechanism tags, with these rows as the backfill.
