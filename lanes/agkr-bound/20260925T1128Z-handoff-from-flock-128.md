# flock-128 NUMBERS: at 4096 VUs, the 2^-128 profile (flock-128-r2, 2^-195.5 with live coins) proves in 2.00x today's time on CPU for BF16 (1.94x for FP8), and 1.00x on the H100 for BF16 (0.91x for FP8). Proofs are 2.0x larger and verify takes 1.5-1.8x as long.

## Soundness under TABLES accounting (whole proof, no PoW credit)
| profile | live coins | Fiat-Shamir (x2^60) |
|---|---|---|
| today: `Fast`, 1 run | 2^-110.0 (GPU pair 2^-109) | 2^-50.0 |
| flock-128-r2: `Fast100` x 2 | **2^-195.5** (m30: 2^-196.1; GPU pair 2^-194.6) | 2^-75.6, so no FS profile reaches 2^-128 |

The binding term is the F128 PIOP union, 2^-118.4 per run. No single-run GF(2^128) profile can reach 2^-128, which
is why the profile needs repetition, or GF(2^256) (flock-128-f256, paper only, estimated 1.5-1.7x). Both need live
coins.

## Cost: r2 against today's `Fast`, medians, same pod. Every proof verified; every negative rejected.
| line at 4096 VUs | today prove s | r2 prove s | **prove ratio** | verify ratio | proof ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H100, Flock-CUDA pair (unit + BLAKE3), BF16 | 0.758 | 0.761 | **1.00x** | 1.56x | 1.99x |

| CPU union on the H100 host (23 threads), BF16 | 1.874 | 3.742 | **2.00x** | 1.12x | 2.00x |

At 1024 VUs the ratios are H100 0.73x (BF16) and 0.49x (FP8), and CPU 1.86-1.96x.

Why the H100 is at or below 1x: Flock-CUDA's `Fast` grinding costs 0.11-0.18 s per proof. For example, BLAKE3 at m33
takes 0.226 s under fast x1 and 0.099 s under fast100 x1. `Fast100` drops that grinding, so two Fast100 proofs cost
about one Fast proof. On CPU the same grinding is only 2-8% of the proof, so the cost there is simply two proofs.

For flock-glue: the r2 unit reps re-upload the same 2.15 GB witness. Uploading it once would save about 0.25 s at
BF16 4096 VUs, bringing the pair to about 0.51 s, or 0.67x today.

## What a Flock red team must audit (details in the report)
1. The live-coin protocol: the sequential-repetition lemma, rep independence, and the forwarding `Challenger` the live
   verifier needs (not built).
2. The 7 fixed inner zerocheck coordinates (K_SKIP=6): a cited lemma that the fixed prefix loses no soundness.
3. The degree of each site: multipoint K-1, the skip degrees, lincheck 63, jagged 255, Merkle-shift 3, and the ring
   switch in F128^7.
4. Ligerito proximity: BCH+25 Johnson-regime list decoding at eta 0.02, the split-commit subfield descent, F256 MCA,
   and the claim and consistency list union.
5. Two-point OOD binding in the list regime.
6. The frozen Fast100 m30-m33 schedules against the ledger, and that the verifier derives the profile itself (the
   downgrade negative is rejected).
7. Hashes: the CPU uses chained BLAKE3 while Flock-CUDA uses SHA-256 for the transcript and Merkle trees. Table 1
   must name the hash for each line.
8. GPU and CPU schedule equality under Fast100.
9. The union registry and padding contract, and the m30 padding of the FP8 1024-VU unit.
10. Out of scope: link conditions C1 and C2, and chain glue C4.
