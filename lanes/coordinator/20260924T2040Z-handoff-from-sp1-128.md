# sp1-128: SP1 cannot honestly reach 2^-128 by raising parameters (only the FRI query round moves; lookup/commit/zerocheck are field-bound at ~2^-100..-112); decision needed before any GPU spend

**Finding (no pod used, $0).** Raising `SP1_TARGET_BITS_OF_SECURITY` only raises the FRI *query-phase* term
(`unique_decoding_queries`, `sp1-primitives` `fri_params.rs`). SP1's other per-shard soundness terms don't depend on it,
and several are bounded by the challenge field KoalaBear^4 (|F| = 2^123.95). Computed with soundcalc (ethereum/soundcalc
@2af5e7d, SP1's own `gen_soundcalc_toml` config, unique-decoding regime, proven bounds from BCHKS25 / Schwartz-Zippel),
log2 of each per-shard error:

| config | query | lookup (LogUp-GKR) | FRI commit rounds (sum) | batching | zerocheck | jagged reduce | **sum** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| stock (124 queries) | -100.1 | -100.3 | -102.4 | -104.4 | -112.2 | -116.4 | **-99.0** |
| target 134 (175 queries) | -134.7 | -100.3 | -102.4 | -104.4 | -112.2 | -116.4 | **-99.9** |
| + GKR_GRINDING_BITS 40, BATCH_GRINDING_BITS 29 | -134.7 | -128.3 | -102.4 | -128.4 | -112.2 | -116.4 | **-102.4** |

- The query figure (-134.7) is a **proven** bound (SP1 uses unique-decoding query counts). It is not the shard's
  soundness: the shard is still 2^-99.9 (proven, additive), or 100 bits by soundcalc's round-by-round minimum.
- No **conjectured** regime reaches 128 either. The lookup, zerocheck and reduce terms are regime-independent
  Schwartz-Zippel terms. Commit-round and batching errors are at least about n/|F| in every regime.
- To reach 2^-128 you'd need grinding in every commit round (about 25 bits at round 1), in zerocheck (about 16) and in the
  jagged reduction (about 12). SP1 implements none of these; they are prover (CUDA) and verifier protocol changes.
  The alternative is a larger extension field. Raising GKR grinding to 40 bits alone costs 2^40 hashes per shard.
- Side note: the existing SP1 D2 rows record per-shard -100 (SP1's constant). The additive figure for stock is -99.0,
  so their union-bounded "achieved" is about 1 bit optimistic (-95.5 recorded vs -94.5 additive at 22 shards). No table
  membership changes.
- Evidence: `lanes/sp1-128/evidence/soundcalc_sp1.py` + `.out` (reproduce: soundcalc @2af5e7d,
  `uv run --with toml python soundcalc_sp1.py`).
