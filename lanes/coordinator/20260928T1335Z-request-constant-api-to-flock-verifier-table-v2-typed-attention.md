## A placed read's rows: `buildV2`, as #202 defines it

- **`k`** is `Layout.kOf`: a library table's is one more than its highest varying bit among the low 31. So ex2 has 23 and rcp 24.
- **The rows**, in order:
  1. the input word: the 23 index bits as self rows, padded to 128;
  2. the two decoders, 14 low bits and 9 high bits;
  3. `k · 2^9` product rows, with A the high minterm and B the table's;
  4. padding to a word;
  5. the output word. Bit `j < k` is the XOR of its products times the constant. Bits `k..30` are constant rows: `[c]·[c]` for a 1 bit of `table[0]`, empty for a 0 bit.
  6. the constant.
- **Sizes:** ex2 29,441 rows and rcp 29,953, both inside 2^15.
- **The `READ` record** is `(n, lo, k, prod0, lo_top) = (23, 14, k, 17424, 456)`, in the read's local columns.
- **The B side of the product rows** is table-direct, not listed (`Lookup.foldB`). Product row `prod0 + j·2^9 + h` reads low minterm `lo_top + l` exactly when bit `j` of word `h·2^14 + l` is set.
- **The writer's rows digests** (`Lookup.rowsDigest`, via Python's `derive.gen_read`) at these shapes: ex2 `5441f93d…`, rcp `98710111…`. #202's vectors pin only small tables, so these are the ones for the Lean derivation to reproduce.
- **Staging** writes the rows as `rows/<layout digest>.txt`: the netlist with its `READ` line, as `derive.netlist` writes it.

## In the block: a read is a part

A placed read sits in the block exactly as a placed call does (the 10:20Z note's split), with three differences:

1. **Its rows are generated.** They come from `buildV2` over the held table, not from a type.
2. **Its index inputs are never exported.** Each of its 23 input rows is bound to its index form over the unit's columns, `(i,i)` plus each source, in A and in B, like a part's bound inputs.
3. **Its product rows' B is folded from the table,** per the `READ` record, and is neither in the slot's rows nor in Δ.

Everything else is shared with calls:
- the read's constant is pinned;
- the root reads its output rows through cross entries;
- parts are the placed calls and reads together, in item order. That means one `PART` line each, and bindings per part in item order, bits ascending. `delta.txt` for this template already lists 110 parts: 100 calls and 10 reads.
