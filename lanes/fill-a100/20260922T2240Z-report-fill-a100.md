## 1. native-peak/v1 — the tool and the three cards

`tools/native_peak/measure.py` (+ `tool.py`, registered as `native_peak`; kind `native-peak/v1` added to `store/kinds.py`; tests in
`tools/native_peak/tests/`, `pytest.importorskip("torch")`). Dense GEMM at 8192³ and 16384³: 3 warm-ups, a 2 s sustained phase (so clocks/power settle),
then median of 5 CUDA-event-timed iterations, `2·N³/s`. BF16 = `torch.matmul` (cuBLAS, fp32 accumulate, `allow_bf16_reduced_precision_reduction=False`,
TF32 off). e4m3 = `torch._scaled_mm(a, bᵀ, scale=1, out_dtype=fp32)` (column-major mat2, as cuBLASLt requires), skipped cleanly below sm_89.
Each size is sanity-checked against an fp32 reference on a 256-row × 256-column block. `nvidia-smi --query-gpu=clocks.sm,clocks.mem,power.draw,
clocks_throttle_reasons.active -lms 100` sampled in the background and windowed per size. `ncu`/`nsys`/`cuobjdump` were not on any pod, so
`sass = "not captured"` everywhere; the executed cuBLAS kernel names (torch.profiler) are recorded instead, and they name the MMA shape.
The headline (top level of `result.json`) is the best size; both sizes are in `sizes`.

Observations worth keeping:
- The A100 sits at its 500 W power cap during the GEMM (throttle reason 0x4 = SW power cap) at 1335–1365 MHz, and delivers 91 % of the 312 TFLOP/s datasheet
  (which assumes 1410 MHz boost: 283.8/312 ≈ 1335/1410 within 4 %). So the campaign's overhead denominator, if it used the *measured* anchor, would be 283.8e12 (overheads ×0.91).
- RTX 4090 BF16 is the odd one: 8192³ (137.4) beats 16384³ (129.4); cuBLAS picks a 64×64-tile CUTLASS kernel (named `…relu…`, identity epilogue) for 8192³ and
  a 256×128 Ampere kernel for 16384³, and the card is power-capped either way. 0.83 of the 165.2 spec.
- **e4m3 "fp32 accumulate" is not IEEE fp32**: the FP8 GEMM output differs from an fp32 reference by 1.44e-4 (8192³) / 1.48e-4 (16384³) relative to max |ref|,
  where BF16→fp32 GEMMs on the same code path land at 2.5e-3 only because the *output* is bf16. 1.4e-4 ≈ 2^-12.8 at K=8192 is the truncated alignment inside
  the tensor-core accumulator, i.e. the same class of effect the campaign's fp8 lanes model. The tool's e4m3 tolerance is 1e-3 (a sanity bound, not an exactness claim); `max_rel_err` is recorded per size.
- Three earlier vy-sp1 launches are failed attempts on the (now terminated) pod, records fetched, not pulled into the store: r20260922-211653-178d (`mem_get_info("cuda")` needs an index — fixed in 043b15d),
  r20260922-211951-0302 (the e4m3 check compared against `b` where the GEMM multiplies by `bᵀ` — fixed in 25f9475), r20260922-212114-4e1e (e4m3 tolerance 1e-4 < the 1.4e-4 truncation error — 915baf9).
