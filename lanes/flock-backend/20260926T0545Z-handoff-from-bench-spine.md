and `art:c4d03dd5` (H100 fp8-hopper K8192). They ran on `write_synth` streams. Those streams can't be turned into spine sets:

- They are drawn from numpy PCG64 (`default_rng([seed, i])`, `_e4m3_operands`). The spec's inputs use label-keyed SHA-256
  counter streams generated by the spine, so no spine recipe reproduces them.
- Their distribution differs from the spine's: uniform bytes with the NaN codes folded to 0x77/0xF7, so those two codes appear
  about twice as often as the other finite codes.
- At each of the four (line, K) pairs, 0 of the first 64 rows coincide with the spine set's, so an instance-equivalence
  document can't exist.
- Their outputs are fine: they are in-domain and evaluate through the IR. Only their provenance fails.
