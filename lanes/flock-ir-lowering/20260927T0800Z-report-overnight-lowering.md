## Findings for owners

- **vLLM FA2 model:** `fa2_model.cpp` `mufu_ex2_bits` shifts a `uint64_t` by 64 or more when |x| < 2⁻⁶³. That is undefined in C++, and x86 wraps the count modulo 64. The IR therefore returns, for example, 1.0083 for ex2(6.5 × 10⁻²²), where hardware gives 1.0. The piece matches the IR as it evaluates; the model should clamp the shift.
- **vLLM sampler:** `TopPMaskWordx{V}` must be made total before it can be lowered.
