## Findings

1. **model5 reproduced on our silicon and harness.** 3001/3001 autoproof dots (both instructions, K up to 256, a different
   RTX 5090 — GPU-66f3ae15… vs autoproof's GPU-297048d1… — a different kernel, driver 570.153.02 vs 570.195.03) and every
   zero-accumulator family of our own (hot clip regime included: 112,226 hot firings, 0 mismatches) match bit for bit. The four
   exact 16-term group dots, the 42-bit window, the 36-bit accumulator window (`acc_window`, `tiny_acc`: 0 mismatches), RZ
   normalisation and the 0x78..0x7E hot-group clip at 2^-19 all hold.
2. **−0 accumulator (new regime):** with all-zero products the hardware returns +0; model5 (autoproof `native.rs`: "accumulator
   unchanged incl. −0") returns −0. 16,328/16,328 such elements. The instruction canonicalises a zero result to +0 regardless of
   the incoming sign.
3. **Opposite-sign accumulator at the window edge (new regime):** 12,790 of 1,507,328 nonzero-accumulator elements differ; in
   the sampled 1,518 mismatches 100% have the accumulator's sign opposite to the result and its msb 37–42 binades below E1 (the
   alignment lsb is E1−41). Hardware magnitude is 1 ulp larger than model5's (2–8 ulps in `large_cancellation`); at distance 42
   it is 1 ulp smaller. autoproof could not see this: in its zero-init chains the accumulator is the running sum, i.e. the
   largest addend, and is never truncated. A narrower accumulator window (best W=38: 1,177/1,518) and an anchor computed from
   the scale exponent fields (927/1,218 fixed, 129/3,300 previously-matching elements broken) both fail, so the accumulator's
   alignment/rounding path (not the term windows) is the open question. Captured in `tiles_*.npz` for the model owner.
4. **MXFP4 (`ue8m0`, `.scale_vec::2X`) is accepted on sm_120a** and executes (`OMMA.SF…E8`). Model5 with a per-32-block shift and
   4 groups of 16 is the better structure (2 groups of 32: 32,691/32,768 wrong in `large_cancellation` vs 126) but not exact:
   798/131,072 zero-accumulator mismatches. Hypothesis open; capture stored.
5. **The RTX 5090 keeps the Hopper BF16 pipeline:** `HOPPER_BF16_M16N8K16` (one 16-wide group, 26-bit window, zero exponent −133)
   reproduces 204,800/204,800 words (randn, uniform bits, cancellation, subnormal); `AMPERE_BF16_M16N8K16` fails 48,569.
