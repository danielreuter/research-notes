# PoUS consolidation: what to merge, fix and remove

This is a read-only review of memory-accounting's code at b87eeef64. It covers `verity/protocols/accounting/space/pous/` (with its `lean/` grader and tests), `verity/Security/{Definitions,Specs,Proofs}/Pous/`, `benchmarks/pous/` and the vLLM PoUS option. Nothing PoUS-derived exists under `experimental/verity_experimental` or `archive/`. Open drafts that change the same ground are noted where they matter: #1159 (P2 RTT cap and `pous/p2/PROTOCOL.md`), #1149 (the lock cut from 104 Pous pins to 11) and #1227 (the Lean-owned timing loop).

The finding that orders everything else is that the tree is still shaped around the band. The band is the registry's `DEFAULT`, vLLM's `DEFAULT_SCHEME`, the generic verifier's defaults and the README's "secure-first default". Since the Feistel10-SHAKE256 Π₂ break (art:7617c8017e80), the band, dense and P3 hold only in the ideal-permutation model, and P2 `p2-16448/v3` (`P2SlackFamilyUncond`) is the sturdiest scheme. The lane's earlier move map (note:20261004T2245Z-report-move-map-pous-answers) sent P2 to `experimental/` and kept the band in `verity/`. The break inverts that: P2 is the scheme that should survive in `verity/`, and the band, dense and P3 become ideal-model references.

## Duplication, and which copy survives

There are two P2 implementations. `schemes/p2.py` is the registry scheme with the certificate. `benchmarks/pous/p2_v1/reference.py` is a second, spec-exact P2 v1 with the setup salt, leaves, root, epoch id and `audit-challenge/v2` key, plus its own `EXPAND_LABEL`, `W_COMMITMENT_LABEL`, `K_AUDIT=105`, `DELTA_NS` and `MAX_RTT_ALLOWANCE_NS`. The scheme's `CERT_K`, `ROOT_CALL_NS` and `EXPAND_LABEL` restate the same constants. The survivor should be one module in the protocol package that follows the spec's framing, with the benchmark importing it. The callers are `p2_v1/{gpu,harness,live}.py`, `bench.py` (`P2V1Gpu`), `benchmarks/pous/tests/test_p2v1*` and the `p2-v1-keys.json` fixture. #1159 adds a test tying the RTT cap in `reference.py` to the spec, and that tie must move with the constant.

There are two P2 codecs. `P2ChaCha` (`p2-16448/v2`) is nonconforming and is kept only for the `p2-gpu` arm. v3 survives. The v2 callers are `schemes/__init__.py` (`REGISTRY`), `schemes/p2.py`, the `p2-16448-v2.json` fixture, `pous_scheme_vectors.py`, 25 references in `test_pous_protocol.py`, `integrations/vllm/tests/protocol_options/test_pous_option.py` (which pins v2 as its experimental example), and the benchmark files `bench.py` (`P2Gpu`), `pod.sh` (`p2-gpu`, whose default is `SCHEME=p2-16448/v2`) and its README. `p2_gpu/p2dec.cu` also holds the tuned squaring that `p2_v1/p2v1.cu` `#include`s. That squaring survives, moved under `p2_v1/`. The ChaCha8 and M2 shared-key paths go.

There are two audit loops. `p2_v1/live.py` hand-rolls its own verifier loop with no `protocol.Verifier` and no certificate refusals, while `band_gpu/live.py` uses `protocol.Verifier`. The `protocol.Verifier` path survives, so P2's benchmark audits refuse what its certificate refuses. `continuous.ContinuousVerifier` wraps `TimedVerifier` rather than duplicating it, so it stays as it is.

There are two codec abstractions. `codec.Codec` (with the `Geometry` protocol) sits beside `protocol.Scheme`, and the generic layer (`audit.HonestResponder`, `setup.server_root`, `verifier.leaves`) reads `scheme.Encoding`, which is P3's module. `protocol.Scheme` survives. `Encoding` moves to a scheme-neutral module (or `layout.py`), and `codec.py` shrinks to what the band and dense references still need.

The Lean has duplicate copies. `Proofs/Pous/Chain/Flat.lean` (53,961 lines, "generated by `flatten_chain.py`", a script that is not in the repo) contains renamed copies (`Pous.X` against `PousX`) of whole standalone modules. These are B5/ExPostFacto (all 50 declarations, with `mem_trace_round` three times), Dense/DenseReCert (21 of 21), P3/Column/BandComb (19 of 19), TwoWay (18 of 18), BandWeights (15 of 15), BandTwoWay (5 of 5), Thm1/Theorem1iiSeq (18 of 18), Theorem1ii (13 of 14), DenseMeets (12 of 13) and Theorem1iTimed (9 of 10). One copy of each should survive. Keeping the standalone modules and making Flat import them is preferable, because nobody can regenerate Flat. Continuous, Graded and Rebuild import `Chain.Family` and Flat, so they move with whichever copy survives. P2's proofs import neither, so P2 does not depend on this cleanup.

The grader duplicates the guarantees. `lean/Grader/Registry.lean` hard-codes 73 `pinnedTargets` that restate names in `Specs/Pous/Guarantees.lean`, including SecureErasure, band, P3 and the open M1 targets. After #1149 the lock keeps 11, so the grader would grade targets the lock no longer pins. The lock should be the one list, with the grader's targets derived from it or from `Pous.Guarantees`.

## Awkward or fragile APIs, and the better form

`audit.TimedVerifier` duck-types its parameters. It takes `call_ns = getattr(vk.params, "call_ns", CALL_NS)` and `k = getattr(vk.params, "challenges", CHALLENGES)`, where the defaults are P3's `CALL_NS=242_000` (not the band's 241,500), `CHALLENGES=132` and `CERTIFIED_ROUNDS=10`, and `WORK=1<<14` (the certificates say 2^20). `P2Params` has neither attribute. So a bare `TimedVerifier` or `ContinuousVerifier` on P2 audits with P3's k and P_T, and converts `LATE_ROUNDS` and `AUDIT_CAP` at 242 µs instead of P2's `ROOT_CALL_NS` (2.9 ms at b87, 854,029 ns after #1159). Its constructor also calls `band.check_deployment`, so the generic loop imports the band. The better form is that `TimedVerifier` takes a `Certificate` (which already carries `k`, `call_ns`, `work` and `cliff`) and has no scheme defaults. `protocol.Verifier` already selects the certificate, and the band's cliff becomes that certificate's `cliff`. The callers are `protocol.Verifier`, `continuous.ContinuousVerifier`, `band_gpu/live.py` (reads `CHALLENGES`), `p2_v1/live.py` (once it uses `protocol.Verifier`), `test_pous_audit.py`, `test_pous_continuous.py` and `benchmarks/pous/tests/test_pous_harness.py`.

`protocol.Verifier(research=..., experimental=...)` uses two flags for one decision, which is which claims a caller accepts. A single accepted-claims set (or one level) would read better. The callers are `band_gpu/live.py`, `p2_v1/live.py` after the merge, and the protocol tests.

`params.py` and `labels.py` are P3 modules that P2 imports for `SALT_BITS`, `tag` and `salt_value`. Those three should move into the scheme-neutral layout module, which `p2.py` and `scheme.py` would then import.

`test_pous_trusted.py` regex-parses the `sha256sum` command out of the grader's README. A small script that both the README and the test call removes the coupling.

The `tests/lean/PousRefCheck.lean`, `PousBandCheck.lean` and `PousDenseCheck.lean` vector checks import POUS Project-store modules (`PebblingMeets.P3Meets`, `BandChainModel`, `PousSponge.SpongeModel`), not `Definitions.Pous`, so they cannot be regenerated in the repo. They should be retargeted at `Definitions.Pous` or retired with their schemes. P2 has no Lean-evaluated vectors at all. `Definitions/Pous/P2/Cost.lean`'s docstring says `p2ring` at φ = id is `schemes/p2.py`'s codec, but nothing executes it against the Python. Under "Lean drives Python", an executable `p2ring` decode with a vector test is the missing piece.

## Dead code to remove

- `benchmarks/pous/hbm_audit/` (`driver.py`, `analyze.py`, README) measured host placement, which died with TwoTierBandwidth. Only `digest.py` is live: `erase_calib/fill.py` uses it through a `sys.path` insert, and `Definitions/Pous/Model/Digest.lean` cites it. Move `digest.py` beside `erase_calib` and drop the rest, along with `hbm_audit/tests` in the benchmark `pyproject` testpaths.
- The `vllm-gpu` arm (`bench.py` `VllmGpu`, `--impl-tree`, `pod.sh`, its README lines, its `test_pous_bench.py` cases) runs a frozen #188 tree through `verity_vllm.engine.pous`, which no longer exists. `band_gpu/` is that code's successor.
- `Proofs/Pous/CheckAxioms.lean` is a `#print axioms` list that `tools/lean/audit.py` makes redundant. It has an exemption in `Proofs/lean-audit.json` and entries in `tools/move/lean_map.toml` and the grader's `check.sh`.
- `Proofs/Pous/Targets.lean` holds `sorry` stubs (A2LemmaAM1, A5RabinGcd, A7Params, M1Meets) under an exemption. It retires with the M1 targets once Daniel rules on M1.
- `CHAIN_CLAIMS` (`schemes/band.py`) and `LOCAL_CLAIMS` (`protocol.py`) still list `feistel-indifferentiability`, which the break refuted. It should become a named ideal-model hypothesis, not a claim the scheme meets. `codec.py`'s docstring still calls dense secure-first.
- The README and `PROTOCOL.md` (706 lines) describe the band as the default, repeat the broken-Π₂ caveat about ten times, and cite pre-move `lean/Pous/...` paths. Under the ruling that an experimental protocol keeps `PROTOCOL.md` only until its Lean covers it, the file shrinks to one caveat and the Security paths, and P2's spec moves to #1159's `pous/p2/PROTOCOL.md`.
- No test replays the round-state (Feistel) break, while `test_pous_narrow_state.py::test_narrow_state_fails_against_the_overwrite_chain` still asserts the chain resists. The test should be relabelled as an ideal-model statement, and a replay of the break should be added beside it, so the break stays pinned.
- `erase_calib/` and the SecureErasure model and proofs are deferred, not dead. They stay frozen and untouched, unless Daniel prefers to archive them.
