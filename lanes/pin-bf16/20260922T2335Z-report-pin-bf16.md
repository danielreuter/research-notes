## New facts about the silicon (BF16, 0 mismatches so these are the model's predictions confirmed word-for-word)

**Accumulator LSB walk (`acc_only_lsb_walk`, 512 tiles × 128, zero products, 64 accumulator bases walked through every bit incl. subnormal,
huge-finite and negative words):** on all four pipelines `D == C` for 65,536 / 65,536 and every one of the 32 bits of D equals the bit of C.
BF16 pipelines pass the whole FP32 accumulator through — all 24 significand bits (and sign/exponent) survive.  Contrast the FP8 pipelines: on
the same H100 run the E4M3 wgmma keeps bits 10–31 only (bits 0–9 match C in exactly 32,768 / 65,536 = chance; `D == C & ~0x3ff` for
262,144 / 262,144 zero-product elements) — the 14-bit adder shows in the accumulator, the 25/26-bit BF16 adders do not.

**Adder-width cancellation (`adder_width_cancellation`, 32 tiles × 128: a cancelling pair ±P with P = 2^(2 pa), pa ∈ {−55, 0, 32, 63}, and
a residual 2^(2pa+ratio), ratio −30…−3, placed in slot k = 2…15; the big term is either the product pair or the accumulator):**

| pipeline | residual in slots 2–7 | residual in slots 8–15 | reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| A100 `sm80` (`ampere_bf16_m16n8k16`, (8,8) / 25) | survives at 2^−24, **dropped at 2^−25** | survives at every tested ratio down to 2^−30 | 25-bit truncating adder over k-groups of 8: the pair in group 0 sets the alignment for slots 0–7 only; a residual in group 1 is aligned to its own group's maximum and is never lost |
| RTX 4090 `sm89` (`ada_bf16_m16n8k16`, (8,8) / 25) | identical to sm80 | identical to sm80 | Ada = Ampere BF16 datapath |
| H100 `sm90` (`hopper_bf16_m16n8k16`, (16,) / 26) | survives at 2^−25, **dropped at 2^−26** | same as slots 2–7 | one group of 16, 26-bit adder; no group boundary anywhere in k |
| RTX 5090 `sm120` (same model) | identical to sm90 | identical to sm90 | consumer Blackwell BF16 mma.sync = Hopper's datapath, now with the width/grouping measured directly (fp4-5090 had 204,800 random elements only) |

Both variants (pair of products vs accumulator as the big term) give the same boundaries, for every pair exponent (P from 2^−110 to 2^126,
residuals down into the FP32 subnormals): the accumulator is aligned with the first group and truncated by the same window.  Also recorded:
`zero_products_nonzero_acc` D == C for 65,536 / 65,536 on all four; `zero_acc_tiny_cancellation`'s zero-exponent probe on the A100 says a
zero accumulator does NOT contribute −126 or −127 to the alignment maximum (163,533 / 87,058 elements would differ; hardware agrees with the
model on all).

## D15(b): the FP8 adder floor — provably unobservable, resolved, −139 kept

`tc_probe.floor_probe_tiles` (family `fp8_floor_probe`, FP8-only, appended last so FP8 tile generation before it is byte-identical — tested):
FP32 accumulators 2^−149 … 2^−126 (every bit of the subnormal range) with all-zero products and with a random zero-product B; n = 1…32 minimal
products ±2^−18 against accumulators in {0, ±2^−149, ±2^−126, ±max_subnormal, 2^−140, 2^−139, 2^−133}; a cancelling ±2^−18 pair over the
same accumulators; the product window 2^−18 … 2^−18−k.  H100 run r20260922-232403-fbd0: 3,584 elements (3,392 nonzero accumulators),
**0 mismatches against `hopper_e4m3_wgmma_k32` with floor −139**, and `floor_alternatives` (the same tiles evaluated under every floor in
[−152, −126], 27 values) gives **3,584 / 3,584 identical words for every floor, all equal to the hardware words**.  The structural reason
(now `resolution` of D15): a floor f acts only when a group's alignment maximum (largest exponent among its products and the incoming
accumulator) lies below f; every nonzero E4M3 product has exponent ≥ −18 and an FP32 accumulator's alignment exponent is ≥ −126 (subnormals
included), so with any nonzero term the maximum is ≥ −126 ≥ f for all f ≤ −126, and with none the sum is 0 for every f.  The fit's lower
bound −126 is exactly the point below which nothing can be observed; a floor > −126 is excluded by the H100 fit (r20260922-183435-fd5d).
`test_no_adder_floor_in_the_h100_fit_range_is_observable_under_the_hopper_fp8_model` proves it for the model.  Model unchanged; D15 `resolved`,
owner pin-bf16, `REGRESSION_TILES["D15"] = (family:fp8_floor_probe, family:zero_products_nonzero_acc)`; `research data refresh-fixtures
--preserve` re-registered `fixtures/discrepancy_log.json` as `art:e960ec04a528595710d77cf274c57edc1a0b9be2d475b64a4a27ef0b93a68a8a`
(previous `art:aac898b7…` kept under `previous`).  Side facts from the same run: n minimal products sum exactly to n·2^−18 (512/512) against
every tiny accumulator; the cancelling pair over any accumulator ≤ 2^−126 returns +0 (the 14-bit window below 2^−18 drops it, floor or no
floor); product window max surviving k = 7, min truncated 8.
