## Leads (not built)

- The mask-rank check (`zk_veil::mask_rank_ok`, single-threaded GF(2) elimination over every mask word) is 2.5 s of main's
  `--zk` at K = 4096 (0.69 s at 14,336: linear in blocks) and 1.6 s over V*'s sessions. Eliminating on a random subset of
  rows first, or over threads, would take most of it off both sides; so would a counter-mode split of level 0's ChaCha draw
  (1.0 s and 0.6 s).
- V*'s per-session inputs are staged in Python here (3-16 min a statement, mostly circuit text that m and the inner's shape fix).
  A native path that writes only the per-session inputs is required before recursion runs in a pipeline.
- Serve's verify of the algebra (6.4 s for a 2-instance statement) and Lean's (192 s) dominate V*'s verify. Most of it is likely
