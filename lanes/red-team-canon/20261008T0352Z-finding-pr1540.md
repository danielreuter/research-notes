## The new `compress` is the old one, everywhere

  equals the three loops as folds over `wordBE`, `schedU` and `roundU`, for every `h`, `data` and `off`, with no
  hypothesis. Its statement is byte-identical at `5058c8b13` and at this head, and so are `wordBE`, `schedU`, `roundU`
  and `U8`. The old body was proved equal to that right-hand side and the new body is now (by `extract_lets`,
  `sched_fold` and `round_fold`), so the two bodies are equal as functions. That is stronger than both being FIPS 180-4
  on well-formed input: the verifier accepts exactly what it accepted before, and gains no form.
- `compress_eq` (`toBV (Sha512.compress h data off) = compressV …`) is byte-identical, statement and proof, and still
  goes through `compress_unfold`. `Hm/Spec.lean`, which defines `compressV`, is untouched.
- `Hm/Row.lean` and `Hidden/Wide/RowCommit.lean` change one `rw` each, using the existing `chain_succ`
  (`Hm/Hash.lean`) instead of an `rfl` that would unfold the new body. No statement changes.
- I read the new body against FIPS 180-4 as well: `sched a b c d` is `W[t-16] + σ0(W[t-15]) + W[t-7] + σ1(W[t-2])`,
  called with `(t-16, t-15, t-7, t-2)` at every `t`, and `round` returns `(t1 + t2, a, b, c, d + t1, e, f, g)`. The new
  definitions (`Regs`, `word`, `sched`, `round`) are an `abbrev` and `@[inline] def`s: no `partial`, `unsafe`,
