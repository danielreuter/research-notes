## Finding 1 (20:40Z): raising SP1's security constant does not give 2^-128

- `SP1_TARGET_BITS_OF_SECURITY` (sp1-primitives `fri_params.rs`; 100 in v6.4.0 f66b4bff5) feeds only
  `unique_decoding_queries(log_blowup) = ceil((T - 16) / -log2(5/8))`: 124 queries at T = 100, 166 at 128, 173 at 133,
  175 at 134. It sets the FRI query-phase term and nothing else.
- SP1's per-shard soundness has other terms, bounded by the challenge field KoalaBear^4 (|F| = 2^123.95) and by two
  grinding constants: `GKR_GRINDING_BITS` 12 (hypercube `verifier/shard.rs`) and `BATCH_GRINDING_BITS` 5 (slop basefold
  `verifier.rs`).
- soundcalc (ethereum/soundcalc @2af5e7d, SP1's own gen_soundcalc_toml config, UDR, proven bounds). Script
  `evidence/soundcalc_sp1.py`, output `evidence/soundcalc_sp1.out`. log2 per-shard error:

| config | query | lookup | commit (sum of 21) | batching | zerocheck | jagged reduce | sum | RBR min (soundcalc total) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| stock, 124 q | -100.1 | -100.3 | -102.4 | -104.4 | -112.2 | -116.4 | -99.0 | 100 |
| T=134, 175 q | -134.7 | -100.3 | -102.4 | -104.4 | -112.2 | -116.4 | -99.9 | 100 |
| T=134 + GKR 40 + BATCH 29 | -134.7 | -128.3 | -102.4 | -128.4 | -112.2 | -116.4 | -102.4 | 103 |

- Proven vs conjectured:
  - The per-shard query figure is a proven bound in the unique-decoding regime (SP1 counts queries for unique decoding).
  - The shard's soundness is proven at 2^-99.9 (additive) for T=134.
  - No conjectured regime reaches 128. Lookup, zerocheck and jagged-reduce are Schwartz-Zippel terms that don't depend on
    the regime. The commit-round and batching errors are at least about n/|F| in any regime.
- 2^-128 would need protocol changes: grinding in each FRI commit round (about 25 bits at round 1), in zerocheck (about 16)
  and in the jagged reduction (about 12), or a larger extension field. Grinding 40 bits for LogUp-GKR alone is 2^40 hashes
  per shard.
- The existing SP1 rows' union-bounded "achieved" (-100 + log2 shards) is about 1 bit optimistic against the additive
  figure (stock -99.0 per shard).
- The soundcalc config carries the v6.1.0 machine sizes (3741 columns, 3412 constraints, 1911 interactions). The 6.4.0
  constants it reads are identical, and a few percent more chips moves these terms by under 0.1 bit.
- Handoff: `lanes/coordinator/20260924T2040Z-handoff-from-sp1-128.md`, asking stop / measure the query cost on A100 as an
