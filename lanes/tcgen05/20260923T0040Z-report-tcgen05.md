## The question and the answer

**Question.** Does datacenter Blackwell's `tcgen05.mma ... kind::mxf4nvf4` (TMEM accumulators, descriptor-fed, different
instruction from the consumer `mma.sync`) produce the same words as the sm_120 model `BlockScaledAlignAdd` that lane fp4-re
pinned on the RTX 5090 (note `r21-silicon/fp4-re/20260922T2230Z-report-fp4-re`)?

**Answer, as evidence.** On one NVIDIA B200 (cc 10.0, GPU-d1d0c3f4, driver 580.126.09, nvcc/ptxas 12.9.86), one
`tcgen05.mma.cta_group::1` (M128 N8 K64, SASS `UTCOMMA`) per output tile, with arbitrary FP32 accumulators preloaded into
tensor memory:

- **sm_100 tcgen05 nvf4** (`kind::mxf4nvf4.block_scale.scale_vec::4X`, UE4M3 scales, SASS `UTCOMMA.4X`) agrees with
  `BLACKWELL_SM120_NVF4` on **16,461,824 words, 0 mismatches**, over the 18 gated nvf4 families of the sm_120 pin
  (`random_random_scales`, `random_zero_acc`, `uniform_encodings`, `scale_sweep_both`, `scale_sweep_a_only`, `hot_clip_regime`,
  `hot_one_block_mixed`, `hot_all_blocks`, `hot_a_only`, `large_cancellation`, `acc_window`, `tiny_acc`, `signed_zero`,
  `signed_zero_matrix`, `acc_offset_walk`, `wide_mantissa_top_scales`, `anchor_participation`, `acc_subnormal`), plus a K=128
  chain (two dependent MMAs on one TMEM accumulator, 1,134,592 words) and autoproof's probe3 vectors **1,601 / 1,601**
  (K=64: 1,057, K=128: 386, K=256: 158, the K>64 cases as dependent MMAs through TMEM).
- **sm_100 tcgen05 mxf4** (UE8M0 scales) agrees with `BLACKWELL_SM120_MXF4` on **12,898,304 words, 0 mismatches**, over the 14
  gated mxf4 families (the 12 shared ones above minus the nvf4-only hot/scale-sweep families, plus `ue8m0_scale_extremes`,
  `ue8m0_underflow`), through **both** mnemonics the toolchain offers for UE8M0 -- `kind::mxf4.block_scale.scale_vec::2X` (what
  CUTLASS 3.9.2's `SM100_MMA_MXF4_SS` emits; 7,913,472 words) and `kind::mxf4nvf4.block_scale.scale_vec::2X` (4,984,832 words incl.
  a 1,028,096-word K=128 chain) -- both lower to plain `UTCOMMA` and both computed the same words.
- `specials` (NaN/Inf/overflow operands, 57,344 words per instruction) is record-only: the model has no defined output there
  (domain errors, D20), exactly as on the sm_120 pin. **Nothing is untested among the pin's families**; what is untested is listed
  under "Scope" below.
- The controls that the sm_120 lane refuted are refuted here by the same counts: autoproof's model5 misses 140,102 / 165,355 words
  of the two big nvf4 runs (fp4-re saw 88,697 / 114,148 on the 5090 at smaller n) and 139,013 / 138,974 / 138,926 of the three
  mxf4 K=64 runs (fp4-re: 139,013 on the identically seeded run -- the B200 words equal the 5090 words there as well); the
  signed-underflow alternative (`BLACKWELL_SM120_MXF4_UNDERFLOW_SIGNED`) misses 14,734 / 14,710 / 14,384 / 5,535, so D13
  (`ue8m0_underflow` -> +0) holds on sm_100.
- Hardware never emitted `-0`: 0 mismatches on `signed_zero` and `signed_zero_matrix` (D16 sign-of-zero holds on sm_100).

So the answer is **yes, the datacenter instruction computes the sm_120 words**, for every family the sm_120 model was pinned on,
with arbitrary finite FP32 accumulators. The registry entries `sm100.tcgen05.mma.e2m1.{nvf4,mxf4}` went `unprobed` -> `pinned`
after `verity.verification.trust` computed PINNED from the labelled evidence (P1..P5 all pass; details below).

## Stage 0: instruction reality on sm_100 (r20260923-001336-5a50, art:3f58060a…12abf)

Toolchain: nvcc 12.9.86 / ptxas 12.9.86 (`Build cuda_12.9.r12.9/compiler.36037853_0`), driver 580.126.09 (nvidia-smi reports
CUDA 13.0), NVIDIA B200 183,359 MiB, compute capability 10.0, image `runpod/pytorch:1.0.3-cu1290-torch280-ubuntu2404`,
CUTLASS v3.9.2 cloned on the pod.

- **`mma.sync kind::mxf4nvf4` does not exist on sm_100.** `ptxas -arch=sm_100a` on the exact PTX the sm_120 harness uses:
  `error : Instruction 'mma with block scale' not supported on .target 'sm_100a'`; the same PTX assembles for `sm_120a`
  (rc 0). Building the sm_120 harness kernel (`mma_fp4.cu`) for sm_100a fails at every `mma.sync ... .block_scale` site and at
  the `kind::f8f6f4` site (`'mma with with FP6/FP4 floating point' not supported`). The PTX ISA statement is confirmed.
- **The tcgen05 probe kernel SASS** (`cuobjdump -sass`, per tile kernel): exactly **1** MMA-class instruction --
  `UTCOMMA.4X` for the nvf4 kernel (744 SASS instructions), `UTCOMMA` for the two mxf4 kernels (888 each) -- plus the tensor-memory
  plumbing `UTCATOMSWS.FIND_AND_SET.ALIGN` (tcgen05.alloc), `UTCBAR` (commit -> mbarrier), `UTCATOMSWS.AND` (dealloc). PTX mnemonics
  in the kernel: `tcgen05.alloc/dealloc/relinquish_alloc_permit.cta_group::1`, `tcgen05.st.sync.aligned.32x32b.x4/x8.b32`,
  `tcgen05.mma.cta_group::1.kind::{mxf4nvf4.block_scale.scale_vec::4X, mxf4nvf4.block_scale.scale_vec::2X, mxf4.block_scale.scale_vec::2X}`,
  `tcgen05.commit.cta_group::1.mbarrier::arrive::one.shared::cluster.b64`, `tcgen05.ld.sync.aligned.32x32b.x8.b32`,
  `tcgen05.wait::ld/st`, `tcgen05.fence::before/after_thread_sync`.
- **CUTLASS 3.9.2 example 72a** (`72a_blackwell_nvfp4_bf16_gemm.cu`, nvfp4 x nvfp4 -> bf16, sm_100a, 99.6 s compile): one kernel,
  **8 x `UTCOMMA.2CTA.4X`** (cta_group::2, 256x256x16-element-block atom `SM100_MMA_MXF4_2x1SM_SS`) with `UTMALDG.3D/4D.MULTICAST.2CTA`
  (TMA loads), `UTCCP.T.S.2CTA.4x32dp128bit` (scale factors smem -> TMEM), `UTCBAR.2CTA.MULTICAST`, `UTMASTG.3D`, `UTMACMDFLUSH`,
  `UTMACCTL.PF`. The probe's `UTCOMMA.4X` is the single-CTA form of the same opcode; **cta_group::2 itself is not probed** (see Scope).
- **nvcc 12.9 gotcha** (recorded as `tcgen05_kernel_builds_with_plain_arch_flag: false`): `nvcc -arch=sm_100a -shared` also embeds a
  plain `compute_100` PTX for the fatbin, and ptxas refuses every tcgen05 mnemonic on `.target sm_100`
  (`'tcgen05.relinquish_alloc_permit' not supported`, `'.cta_group::1' not supported`, ...). `-gencode=arch=compute_100a,code=sm_100a`
  builds cleanly (2.4 s). The harness (`probe.py build`, `stage0_sm100.py`) now emits explicit gencode for every `sm_XXXa` target.

## Stage 1 + 2 together: one `tcgen05.mma` per tile with an arbitrary TMEM accumulator (tools/tc_probe_fp4/tcgen05_fp4.cu)

The spec's Stage 1 (CUTLASS/cuBLASLt with beta=0) was skipped in favour of going straight to the Stage-2 kernel: a library GEMM
zero-pads K, runs its own epilogue and hides the accumulator, so it would have needed exactly the contamination analysis the spec
warned about while proving less. The hand kernel needs no CUTLASS at all (raw PTX, 336 lines) and gives every family:

- Harness tiles are 16x8xK=64 (the sm_120 shape). `tcgen05.mma` is M=128 minimum, so each 16-row tile is **replicated into all 8
  groups of 16 TMEM lanes**; the 8 replicas are compared word for word (`replica_disagreements = 0` in every run, both paths).
- Operands: A (128x64 E2M1) and B (8x64 E2M1) as K-major, no-swizzle smem descriptors (LBO 128 B, SBO 256 B; both strides are
  runtime-configurable via `--smem-strides`); scale factors (UE4M3 4X or UE8M0 2X per 32/64 K) in TMEM; instruction descriptor
  `0x08020480` (nvf4: e2m1 x e2m1 -> f32, UE4M3, M128 N8, K-major) / `0x08820480` (UE8M0).
- Accumulator: preloaded into TMEM with `tcgen05.st` and consumed with `enable_input_d = 1`. All-+0-accumulator tiles run with
  `enable_input_d = 0` (the hardware's own "no incoming accumulator" path) *and* -- in the `tmem_path` pass of every run -- through
  the TMEM preload; `path_disagreements = 0` everywhere (the two paths compute identical words), so the beta=0 contamination question
  is answered at the instruction, not at an epilogue.
- One MMA issued from one thread (`#pragma unroll 1` keeps the SASS at one `UTCOMMA` even with the chain loop), `tcgen05.commit` ->
  mbarrier, readback with `tcgen05.ld`. `--chain2` issues two dependent MMAs on the same TMEM accumulator for K=128 tiles (the model
  is stepped twice, feeding the first block's word into the second), which is the spec's "K>64 accumulates in TMEM" test done
  deliberately.
- A layout check (accumulator disabled, TMEM path, chain) against exact A.Bt gates every run before the sweep (`layout_check: passed`).

## Scope: what this pins and what it does not

- Pinned: `tcgen05.mma.cta_group::1`, M128 N8 K64, K-major no-swizzle smem operands, scale factors in TMEM, any finite FP32
  accumulator, UE4M3 (4X) and UE8M0 (2X, both mnemonics), both K=64 single MMAs and K=128 dependent chains through TMEM.
- Not probed (and not claimed): `cta_group::2` (`UTCOMMA.2CTA`, what CUTLASS's 2-SM atoms emit -- the same opcode family, but the
  paired-CTA accumulator split is a different data path), N > 8 and swizzled / MN-major operand layouts (numerics should not depend on
  them, but that is an assumption until probed), `kind::f8f6f4` on tcgen05 (autoproof's probe2 path; skipped, no FP8 tcgen05 backend
  here), sm_103 (B300), a second B200 device / driver (S1).
- `specials` and NaN/Inf/overflow accumulators are outside the model's domain on sm_100 exactly as on sm_120 (D20).

## What the spec got wrong / things to know

- `mma.sync kind::mxf4nvf4` on sm_100a: the spec asked to confirm it does not assemble -- confirmed, and the *harness kernel itself*
  cannot even be built for sm_100a, so the sm_120 backend is not a fallback on datacenter parts.
- Stage 1 via CUTLASS/cuBLASLt was the wrong first step: a library GEMM pads K, applies an epilogue and hides the accumulator; the
  raw-PTX kernel was faster to write than to de-contaminate a library path and covers Stage 2 directly. CUTLASS was still used, for
  the stage-0 SASS of 72a.
- `nvcc -arch=sm_100a` is not enough in 12.9: it also compiles a `compute_100` PTX that refuses tcgen05. Use
  `-gencode=arch=compute_100a,code=sm_100a` (the harness does now). The 5090 lane's `-arch=sm_120a` happened to work because the
  block-scaled `mma.sync` is accepted by the `compute_120` target.
- `ANCHOR_SKU` already had sm_100 -> B200; nothing to add.
- `tcgen05.mma`'s minimum M is 128, so a 16-row harness tile costs 8x replication; that is free (the replicas double as a
  self-consistency check) but any future "one instruction per output element" claim on sm_100 should say M128 N8 K64.
- UE8M0 on tcgen05 has two mnemonics (`kind::mxf4 2X` and `kind::mxf4nvf4 2X`); CUTLASS uses the former. Both were probed because a
  pin on one mnemonic would not have covered the code CUTLASS emits.
- B200 availability on RunPod was the schedule risk (~36 min of "no stock" before one appeared); the pod-hour budget was not.
- `research data snapshot --members` takes one comma-separated argument (or repeated flags), not a space-separated list.
